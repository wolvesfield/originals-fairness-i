Mines Fairness Verifier

Recompute a settled round from a revealed seed and confirm it was fair.

Verification only. This recomputes outcomes of already-settled rounds from a revealed server seed. It does not predict future rounds — provably-fair outcomes are HMAC-SHA256 output and carry no exploitable pattern. There is no "next outcome" to compute until the seed is revealed, which only happens after those rounds have settled.
