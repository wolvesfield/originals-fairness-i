## 2025-02-28 - Avoid Sets and Dynamic Iterators in Hot Loops
**Learning:** In highly repetitive loops (like Web Worker brute-forcing via `SCAN_CHUNK` or fairness generators), dynamically instantiating `new Set()` and calling `Array.from()` causes severe garbage collection overhead. Profiling showed that avoiding GC by hoisting and reusing global pre-allocated typed arrays (e.g., `Uint8Array` for hit detection, `Int32Array` for tracking shuffles) can yield measurable execution time improvements (around 5-15%).
**Action:** When working on brute-forcers or Monte Carlo simulators, avoid abstractions like `.filter()`, `.includes()`, `new Set()`, and `Array.from()`. Pre-allocate fixed-size typed buffers at the module scope, reuse them across executions by overwriting state (`.fill(0)` or copying indices), and use manual `for` loops for iteration and copying. Ensure fairness engines stay synchronized with workers using this pattern.
