## 2024-03-24 - Cryptography Hot Path Optimization
**Learning:** The use of `CryptoJS.HmacSHA256().toString(CryptoJS.enc.Hex)` combined with `slice()` and `parseInt()` in the deterministic float and crash point generation algorithms (which are called millions of times in Monte Carlo simulations and worker pools) creates a massive garbage collection footprint and string allocation overhead.
**Action:** Instead of instantiating new HMAC objects and converting output to hex strings, use `CryptoJS.algo.HMAC.create()` to cache an HMAC instance, and then use `.reset()` and `.update()`. Furthermore, extract deterministic numbers directly from the `finalize().words` array using bitwise shifts (e.g. `hashWords[0] >>> 0`) rather than parsing a hex string, resulting in ~2.5x speedup.
