## 2025-02-12 - Keno Algorithm Optimization (Uint8Array Map vs Set)
**Learning:** `new Set()` instantiations inside highly repetitive loops (like Monte Carlo simulations and collision avoidance algorithms in `aimingWorker.ts` and `fairnessEngine.ts`) incur massive overhead. In a benchmark, utilizing a `Uint8Array` as a map/cache instead of a `Set` for domains with small continuous integer keys (like Keno domains up to 40) is ~12x faster. Furthermore, generating numbers inside the evaluation loop rather than fully computing arrays and then validating allows for early-exiting when hits are met (`validateKenoState`).
**Action:** When evaluating collision-based checks across small domains in hot loops within this codebase, always substitute `Set` usage with simple `Uint8Array` allocations and early-exits for optimal performance. Watch out for unintended `git` modifications like `pnpm-lock.yaml` generation during testing and revert them carefully to keep PR clean.
