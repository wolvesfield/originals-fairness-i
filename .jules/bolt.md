## 2024-04-16 - Array.includes vs Sets and Loops on small arrays
**Learning:** In the hot loops of the headless endpoints and seed engineering (`src/server/express.ts`, `src/analysis/HeuristicSeedEngineer.ts`, `src/analysis/ClientSeedOptimizer.ts`), replacing `Array.includes()` with `Set` is actually *slower* for very small arrays (e.g. mines `[2, 10, 24]` and targetTiles `[10, 11, 12, 13]`). The overhead of instantiating `new Set()` inside the loop vastly outweighs the O(1) lookup benefit when N is small.
However, replacing `.some(t => mines.includes(t))` with a manual nested loop with early break reduces the scan time by roughly 60%.
**Action:** For highly repetitive tight loops involving very small arrays (N < 50), do not instantiate Sets. Use manual nested `for` loops with early breaks, which avoids function call overhead and instantiation entirely. Pre-computing Sets or Bitmasks outside the loop is only effective if the object lives across many iterations (e.g. Anchor Rounds in Seed Engineer), and manual nested loops can sometimes outperform even Sets if arrays are tiny.
